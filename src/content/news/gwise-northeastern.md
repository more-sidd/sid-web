GWiSE — Graduate Women in Science and Engineering — is Northeastern's
graduate community for women across engineering and the sciences, running
mentorship, outreach and social programming across the college.

I've been involved with the group through my time in the robotics programme.
Graduate engineering can be isolating, and a room full of people who have
already navigated the same lab politics, the same imposter feelings and the
same job search is worth a great deal more than another networking event.
