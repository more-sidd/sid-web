Graduate Women in Science and Engineering (GWiSE) is Northeastern's student-led
community supporting women in graduate STEM programs through peer mentorship,
K-12 outreach, and social gatherings.

Since joining the robotics program, I have been active in GWiSE both as a member
and by helping lead social media and outreach for the club. When you spend most
of your week debugging code or working late in the lab, grad school can start to
feel lonely fast. What makes GWiSE different from a typical professional mixer
is honesty: you get to talk openly with people who have already survived the
same classes, advisor dynamics, and tough job markets, and who genuinely want to
see you succeed.
