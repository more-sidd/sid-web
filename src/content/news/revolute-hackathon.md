**August 1 to 2, 2026**

I co-organized Revolute, Boston's first hands-on robotics and physical AI
hackathon, hosted at FabLab Kendall in Cambridge in partnership with Boston
Robot Hackers. Organizing this event gave me direct experience coordinating
hardware logistics, designing technical imitation learning workshops, and
building a local engineering community from the ground up.

## Project Overview and Goal

Boston has a massive robotics industry, yet there were few casual, hands-on
spaces for builders to actually touch physical AI hardware without working at
a lab or startup. The goal was to bring 30 engineers and students together for
a weekend to train and deploy real manipulation policies around a fun, tactile
theme: cooking with robot arms.

## Event & Hardware Setup

- **Real Hardware Over Simulation:** Partnered with Seeed Studio to put roughly
  $15,000 of physical hardware directly in participants' hands, moving straight
  past simulation into real-world debugging.
- **Team Workstations:** Equipped each team with a reBOT Arm B601 RS (48 V
  quasi-direct-drive arm with a 2.5 kg payload), a leader arm for teleoperation
  and imitation learning, an 8 GB reComputer Jetson Orin Nano for on-edge
  inference, and dual USB cameras (overhead and wrist-mounted).
- **Weekend Structure:** Kicked off with a hands-on hardware and software
  tutorial, moved into open hacking, and wrapped with a science-fair-style demo
  floor where teams submitted a GitHub repo, documentation, and a one-minute
  demo video.
- **Industry Judging:** Brought in practicing engineers from Autodesk, Minoic
  Intelligence, Elemental Machines, Walden Robotics, and Vassar Robotics to
  evaluate the builds and give direct technical feedback.
